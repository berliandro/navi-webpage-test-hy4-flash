## Direction contract

THESIS: The team is the shirt every fan wears and shops for. The category-default fan site — hero photo, row of equal player cards, merch grid — is refused. The page is one garment seen once, at one scale, from one point of view: its chest carries the identity and proof, its woven tag carries the roster, its care label carries the action.

OWN-WORLD: Cut-and-sew construction as the recognizable language with all content removed — black and off-white curved panels, visible seam lines and garment-tape edges, one NAVI-yellow piping thread as the only signal colour. Heat-pressed condensed capitals for the team name and proof; a narrow vertical woven tag column sets tiny condensed player names and one-word roles; tabular figures only where a verified number appears. No gradients, glass, generic icon tiles, or stock component shells.

STORY: The visitor reads NAVI as something you wear and belong to, not a case study. They see the seven verified names on the woven tag, the S18 runner-up proof heat-pressed on the chest panel, and the current lineup in one glance, then follow the stitched Shop action to a directory of real merchandise where official and fan-made goods are visibly separated. Fan-made goods are never presented as endorsed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed panelled jersey fills the fold at 1440×810. A top rail sets the NAVI mark and plain links Roster, Matches, Titles, Media, Shop. The chest panel carries NAVI MPL ID in heat-pressed condensed caps and one line of verified proof: MPL ID Season 18 runners-up. A vertical woven tag at the right edge lists the seven players with the one-word roles JUNGLE, EXP, MID, GOLD, ROAM and a staff line. One unmistakable NAVI-yellow stitched Shop the collection tab sits at the tag's foot. A slim verified results strip runs along the bottom edge and the next section starts below it. On phones the panels stack and the tag becomes a horizontal hem band; the Shop action stays above the fold.

FORM: The Cut-and-Sew Jersey — position 1 of the grounded list, locked from the IMPECCABLE'S PICK card; `seed:7d5bf508817409da`. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.webp` (prompt sidecar marked approved) is the finish-review critique reference.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Scope and boundaries: keep all verified content; no invented roster facts, jersey numbers, stats, testimonials or endorsements. The shop is an outbound directory, not a checkout. Keep self-hosted assets and no hosted font stylesheet. Do not restyle the old pinned world — replace it. Visible role vocabulary stays one word: JUNGLE, EXP, MID, GOLD, ROAM.

States and ranges: realistic content — 7 players + staff, 19 verified fixtures, verified awards and a partial stat set (Febbb has no published figures → designed no-data state). Merchandise: 36 sourced images, official and fan-made, each linking to its real seller page.

Interaction and layout: the garment is the page's carrier, not a picture of a garment. Navigation reads as links on the panels, the roster reads as set text on the tag, and the Shop action is plainly clickable. ## Locked addition: achievement crests

Direction locked by the user as **Crest — Study 2** (seed reference `seed:b12961d5b18c749f`; concept roll plus rerolls and icon studies in `.impeccable/mocks/decision/`). The user rejected the vertical medallions and the flat icon-grid treatment as too plain: the second reference supplies only the icon vocabulary (bold, readable glyphs with block shadows); the first (halftone "Z") supplies the treatment.

**Revision after user critique:** the first build was judged messy — the plaque sat beside rather than behind the icon, the type was oversized and shouty, the yellow border was loud, the detail line repeated the title, the unit covered the player's face and wasted the field to its right, and every honour shared the same shield silhouette. The shipped language now applies the brutalist treatment to **four distinct abstract silhouettes** — a crown, crossed blades, a compass star and a trophy cup. The plaque is a quiet 1px seam rule behind the icon with off-white 800-weight title, faint 700-weight detail and a single 2px thread tick; no notch, no bright border. The unit sits in the clear field to the right of the cutout, clear of the face and the woven tag; on phones it collapses to one compact honour above the head, clear of the cloth proof. Content stays the four verified S18 honours; detail lines say only `S18 · Individual` or `S18 · Team`, and rarity is artwork weight, never an invented tier.

**Icon production (revised):** the earlier VectorCraft set was removed at the user's direction — its drawing quality sat below this site's standard. The four shipped crests are now **sourced glyphs by Lorc on Game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0)**: Spiky explosion (Rookie), Crossed swords (First Team Jungler), Compass (First Team Roamer) and Trophy (S18 runner-up). Each source path is recoloured into the same system language — a NAVI-thread (#ffd400) halftone fill on a 45° screen, a 16px ink (#14150f) outline on the 512-unit viewBox and a hard block shadow applied in CSS — so outline weight, fill treatment and drawing style stay consistent across all four. Self-hosted SVGs ship in `public/assets/achievements/` with source links, licence and change notes in that folder's `PROVENANCE.md`. The roster renders them through `asset()` so they work under the GitHub Pages base path. The notification stack was reduced to 60/68px icons and raised to the chest edge so it clears the player's head.

**Layout correction (grid + hardening):** the honours are a semantic `<ul>` placed as a child of `.chest`, not `.print`, so they anchor to the garment's column grid — right-aligned to the chest edge with the `--honour-inset` gutter, clear of the woven tag. Each honour is one grid row of `var(--honour-icon) minmax(0,1fr)`: the icon track and the badge size are identical for every honour, so all badge centres share one x. The plaque starts at the badge centreline (`margin-inline-start: calc(var(--honour-icon) / -2)`) with `border-inline-start: 0`, so the icon (one layer above) masks the join and the bar reads as extruded from the badge with no visible left edge; the decorative yellow tick was removed. Hardening: long titles ellipsize instead of overflowing, the detail line ellipsizes, the crest keeps a fallback monogram if the image fails, the stack leaves the accessibility tree when Stats is open, and a forced-colors outline survives flattening. Mobile keeps the same grid with smaller shared tokens and shows only the lead honour.
