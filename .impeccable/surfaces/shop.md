# Shape brief: Shop (NAVI fan hub)

Assumptions asserted from `PRODUCT.md`, `PLAN.md` and `public/assets/merch/PROVENANCE.md`.

1. **Job and audience:** A fan who just learned about the team wants real merchandise. Persuade mode. They must be able to tell official from fan-made at a glance and leave for the seller's own page.
2. **Outcome and proof:** Success is an outbound click to a real, verified seller URL. Proof: 36 real product images (1024×1024 or 1000×1000) and a provenance table with item name, official/fan classification, direct image, source page and resolution. No in-site checkout, cart, price invention or endorsement claim.
3. **Selected direction:** Inherits the Cut-and-Sew Jersey world. The shop reads as a garment rack / care-label directory: the same panelled fabric, one yellow piping signal, tabular product codes, and a visible thin border + label separating official from fan-made goods. Fan-made goods are never presented as endorsed. Approved comp for the world: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.webp`.
4. **Scope and boundaries:** Replace the stub `app/shop/page.tsx` with a client component carrying the sourced catalogue. Use only images and URLs from `public/assets/merch/PROVENANCE.md`. Keep self-hosted assets and the shared `SiteHeader`/`SubPage` shell restyled into the new world. No fabricated prices; show published names and a source link, with a classification badge.
5. **States and ranges:** 36 sourced items (official: home/away 2026 jerseys, player-issue variants, official-store listings; fan: jerseys, jackets, tees across Shopee/ThaiPick). Realistic mix of product names with long or non-Latin characters. Filter states: All / Official / Fan-made, with visible counts. Designed empty state if a filter yields nothing. Outbound links open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
6. **Interaction and layout:** A filter rail sets the lens; each card shows the real image, item name, classification label and an outbound "View at seller" action. Cards sit on fabric panels with a thin separating border; official and fan-made are visibly distinguished. Responsive from 1440 to 390 with no clipping; keyboard focus visible on every control.
7. **Constraints and open decisions:** Every outbound URL must come from the provenance file and resolve; do not invent a repo URL for deployment. Delivery via static export. Open: none that block this surface.
