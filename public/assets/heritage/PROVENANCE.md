## Org-level facts used on the Titles page

All chapter facts come from the cited public record. No season-specific claim from a later MPL season is added to this project.

| Year | Fact | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2009 | Natus Vincere formed on 17 December in Kyiv; the name means "born to win". | [Natus Vincere — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natus_Vincere) |
| 2010 | ESWC world champion, World Cyber Games world champion and DreamHack Winter winner; first team to hold IEM, ESWC and WCG at once. | [Natus Vincere — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natus_Vincere) |
| 2011 | Won the first The International (Dota 2), beating EHOME 3–1 in the grand final for $1,000,000. | [Natus Vincere — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natus_Vincere) |
| 2021 | Won PGL Major Stockholm 2021, the organisation's first CS:GO Major, 2–0 over G2; completed the Intel Grand Slam. | [Natus Vincere — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natus_Vincere) |
