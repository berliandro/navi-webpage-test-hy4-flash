## Shipped use (fan-hub Shop)

All 36 images ship locally from `public/assets/merch/` and are rendered by `app/shop/page.tsx` from the typed catalogue in `app/merch.ts`. That catalogue is generated from the table above by `scripts/build-merch.mjs`; regenerate it if this file changes. There is no in-site checkout, no invented price and no endorsement claim. Images remain the property of their respective owners; this project claims no rights over them.
